# NotebookLM and experimental features

The 2026-10-17 source treated NotebookLM as a separate research/knowledge service that may be linked from Gemini, with account-specific notebook and source limits. Exact limits and UI placements can change.

## Notebook workflow

1. Create a notebook for a defined research question.
2. Upload or link **authorized** sources; distinguish source quotations from synthesis and speculation.
3. Ask focused questions and check citations against source passages.
4. If generating audio/presentation-style outputs, download and verify the resulting artifact rather than assuming a history entry is permanent.
5. Record the dates and sources of any factual claims included in a public-facing summary.

## Labs

Check account/region availability, version and any experimental warnings. Do a low-risk trial before depending on a Labs feature for production. Never claim an experimental toggle is universally available.
