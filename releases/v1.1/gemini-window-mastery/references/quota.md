# Usage-limit observations and reporting format

Source date: **2026-10-17**. The private field notebook described a five-hour meter, a weekly meter, model/feature-specific capacities and image/music/video quotas. These are **account-specific dated observations**, not universal product guarantees. Do not copy its private account figures or claim any fixed per-day image or token entitlement.

## Reading the current state

1. Check the active account/profile locally.
2. Open the current settings/usage/limits UI if that panel exists in the receiving product.
3. Record the exact model or feature, meter type (e.g. rolling window, weekly budget, daily allowance), percent or count, reset timestamp, time zone, and capture time.
4. If a limit is absent or undocumented, mark it `UNKNOWN`, not `unlimited`.
5. Recheck after a representative operation and keep separate rows for images, music, video and reasoning if the UI distinguishes them.

## Shareable evidence row

`account_alias | feature_or_model | task_type | usage_meter | observed_value | reset_time_and_tz | observed_at | output_verified`

Examples use fictional account aliases; do not commit real addresses or screenshots revealing billing profiles. Say `video-generation feature limited until [observed time]`, not `the whole account is out of quota`.
