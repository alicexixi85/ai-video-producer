# Quality Gates

1. Canon: every event and character decision comes from the source script or story bible.
2. Identity: cast IDs, faces, hair, age and clothing are consistent.
3. Timeline: durations have no gap, overlap or frame-rate discrepancy.
4. Visuals: a shot has one primary action and specific prompts.
5. Dialogue: no unsupported dialogue is inserted.
6. Audio: cues fit the video timeline and do not claim nonexistent audio assets.
7. Captions: subtitles reflect the spoken words and language.
8. Handoff: missing assumptions are explicit, and a shot plan is not labeled a rendered video.

An independent reviewer should check story continuity and production quality.
